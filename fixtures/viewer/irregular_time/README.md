# Irregular exact-epoch viewer fixture

Synthetic, repository-authored input under Apache-2.0. Elapsed times are exactly 0, 1 and 10 seconds, each preserving an epoch timestamp ending in 1 ns. The body rotates about +X while the raw sign alternates. The +X trail stays fixed while +Y and +Z change. The time axis must place the middle sample one tenth of the way across, and the sign-lift candidate must preserve physical orientation. This is a visual-QA fixture, not operational telemetry.
