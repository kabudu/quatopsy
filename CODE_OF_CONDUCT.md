# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Quatopsy respectful, safe, and productive for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, precise, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome questions and explain specialist terminology when it helps others participate.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, correct mistakes, and keep safety-critical claims proportionate to evidence.
- Protect private, proprietary, and operationally sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal attacks, sustained disruption, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, and retaliation are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a GitHub private security advisory titled `Code of Conduct report`. Do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inconsistent with this code. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and impartially as practical. Knowingly false or bad-faith reports may themselves violate this code.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for Quatopsy's small, safety-conscious engineering community.
